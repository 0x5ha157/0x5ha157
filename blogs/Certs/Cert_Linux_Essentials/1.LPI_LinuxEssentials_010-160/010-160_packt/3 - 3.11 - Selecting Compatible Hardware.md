## Selecting Compatible Hardware
##### LPI Linux Essentials (010-160)

### Objectives

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Locate the vendor approved lists of supported hardware.
2. Identify whether hardware is supported under Linux. 
3. Identify companies that sell PCs with Linux support.
