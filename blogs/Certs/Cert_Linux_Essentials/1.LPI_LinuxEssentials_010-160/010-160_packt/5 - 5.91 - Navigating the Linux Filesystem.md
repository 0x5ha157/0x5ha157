## Navigating the Linux Filesystem
##### LPI Linux Essentials (010-160)

### Objectives

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Describe the Linux Filesystem Hierarchy Standard (FHS)
2. Identify the key folders defined by the Linux FHS. 
3. Identify variations to the FHS as implemented by Red Hat and Canonical.
